Chain of Responsibility Pattern

4-Step Matching Pipeline

Four handlers are chained. Each evaluates the candidate and either rejects or passes them forward.

🩸
Step 01
Blood Type Matcher
ABO compatibility gate. Exact match +20, compatible +15. Mismatch is immediately rejected.
🧬
Step 02
HLA Tissue Matcher
HLA scoring — below threshold is rejected. Proportional score up to +40.
📍
Step 03
Geographic Filter
Haversine-formula distance check. Beyond radius is rejected. Proximity up to +15.
🚨
Step 04
Urgency Evaluator
CRITICAL +25 • HIGH +15 • MEDIUM +10 • LOW +5. Highest composite wins.
